# Code of conduct

lephycrypy+ welcomes everyone who wants to use or improve it, whatever their background or
experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Accept that a security project says "no" or "not like this" more often than others, and
  explain your own "no" with reasons.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's personal data, files, passwords or keys.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, threats, publishing
private information, sexualised content, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other project space. The
maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, temporarily block or ban participants
who do not follow it. Report problems privately to the maintainers through their GitHub
profiles; reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
